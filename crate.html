Texture Crate

Collect textures from ambientCG (CC0, free for any use) and download only what you need. Works offline except for the downloads themselves. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

1. Get a list

Option A: paste what Claude gave you

Paste the JSON block (a manifest or palette) and press Load.

Option B: browse the catalog yourself

Open ambientcg_catalog.csv (included, or rebuilt with the catalog script).

2. Your crate

If your browser asks to allow multiple downloads, choose Allow. Each row also has its own link. Files arrive as .zip in your Downloads folder; unzip them into your project.

Notes

  • HDRIs and 3D models use different file names than materials (for example 1K-HDR). If a download fails, ask Claude for the exact file name, or copy it from the asset's page on ambientCG.
  • Credit is not required for CC0 assets, but ambientCG appreciates it. See their license page.
  • This tool is a community project and is not affiliated with ambientCG.
